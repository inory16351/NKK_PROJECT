LAYOUT — an exact grid: 4 columns x 2 rows, each cell 384x512. Each drawing centered in its own cell with a clear magenta margin; nothing touches the cell borders. The 4th column is ONE tall cell (384x1024) for the ladder.

Column 4 (one tall cell, full image height):
